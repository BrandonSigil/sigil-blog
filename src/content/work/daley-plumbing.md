## Plumber for the people

The brand is a little retro: a looping script logotype, condensed all-caps headlines and a blue duotone over real jobsite photos. It reads like a local crew, not a franchise.

## Pages that rank

Every service and every South Sound city gets its own page, with its own copy and LocalBusiness schema. A call button sits at the top of every mobile screen, because most visitors are standing in a puddle when they find the site.

## Fast by default

Like everything we ship, it's static HTML from Astro on Vercel. It loads instantly on a phone in a basement with one bar of signal.

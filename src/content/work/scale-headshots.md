## The problem

Company headshot day is a scheduling headache. Someone is always out sick, new hires join the week after, and remote staff never make it. You end up with a team page full of mismatched photos. Scale, a joint product from Snapbar and Helium, takes the photographer out of the loop and keeps the consistency.

## Brand

The S mark is two interlocking arcs, like a lens turning. It sits on soft blooms of blue, volt and orchid that hint at studio lighting without spelling it out. The palette is bright enough for a product launch and calm enough for enterprise procurement decks.

## Product

The capture flow is mobile-first because that's where it happens. The admin side is a single queue built for one job: getting a few hundred people approved before the website update ships. Every screen leads with the photo itself.

## Marketing site

The site is built in Astro and organized by company size. Startups, small businesses, growing teams and enterprises each get their own landing page, pricing story and proof points. A developer section sells the API on its own terms, with real request examples.

## The mark

The monogram is a T drawn as a house in profile, with a roofline, a gable and a solid wall. It's simple enough to cut in vinyl, stamp on a hard hat or read at 45 mph. We pushed through six rounds, trying outlines, wood grain and split fills, before settling on a black mark with an orange keyline.

## Color

One color earns its keep: Safety Orange, #EF681E. It's the color of cones and vests, and it pops against both white trailers and black trucks. Everything else is asphalt black and concrete grey.

## In the field

We mocked the identity up on a real 7×14 enclosed trailer at actual artwork size before signing off. One option runs a clean lockup with the phone and URL, the other goes oversized with the mark alone. Business cards come in orange, black and a wood-texture edition for the crew.

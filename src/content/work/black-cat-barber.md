## Character first

Most barbershop sites are a price list and a map. Black Cat has a real point of view, so the site opens on a green duotone of the shop's own Felix collection and gets out of the way. The headline, *You'll forget your hat*, sets the tone.

## Built light

It's one fast, static page built with Astro: services, team, shop and contact. Booking hands off to the shop's existing system. Merch runs through Gumroad with Printful printing on demand, so there's no inventory to manage. Analytics are cookie-free.

## Merch

The shop section turned the brand into something you can take home: records, tees and prints, printed and shipped on demand.

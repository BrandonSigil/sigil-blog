## Tone

*There is peace on the other side of overwhelm.* The headline sets the tone for the whole site. It's a high-contrast serif with one word in terracotta, plenty of cream space, and no stock-photo smiles.

## One next step

Every page leads to the same button, *Book your free consultation*. The process is spelled out in three plain steps (walk through, work side by side, live in it) so a first call feels low-stakes.

## Details

Service pages cover decluttering, organizing, downsizing and moving. They're written in Jules's voice, judgment-free, and short enough to read on a phone in a cluttered kitchen.

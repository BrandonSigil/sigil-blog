## The brief

Helium's apps are used by thousands of Shopify merchants, including brands like KITH, Stanley and L'Occitane. The 2025 refresh had to look at home next to those names without losing the warmth that makes the apps feel approachable.

## The idea

We kept the atom mark and gave it room to breathe. Everything sits on soft, out-of-focus color, as if you're looking through glass. That lets the type do the talking. Headlines are set in a monospaced face because the company writes software, and they're kept short:

- *Made from matter.*
- *Tools for tomorrow.*
- *Experts don't need to shout.*

## The system

Two colors do most of the work, Matter Pink and Volt Violet, grounded by a near-black plum. Posters use a fixed grid with the wordmark rotated up the right edge, so every piece reads as Helium before you've read a word. The same pieces scale down to app icons and social tiles and up to billboards, the website hero, and swag.

## One template, every Friday

Every cover uses the same stacked masthead, *NEW / MUSIC / FRIDAY*, locked to the top-left, with a small date tag in the corner. The rest of the frame belongs to that week's lead album: cropped, darkened and sometimes tinted toward Sigil green.

## Reviews

Full album reviews get their own treatment: the cover art floats on a green gradient plinth like a record on a shelf. They're quiet next to the loud NMF covers, which is the point.

## Why it works

Constraints made it sustainable. The type never moves, so nobody had to make design decisions at 8 a.m. on a Friday, only pick the album.
